Specialized Technical Capabilities

Civil Engineering & High-Tension Aerial Infrastructure

Underground Civil Trenching & Duct Installation

Precision micro-trenching and Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD) executed to protect municipal infrastructure. We install heavy-duty HDPE SDR conduit systems below regional frost lines with minimal surface disruption.

  • • Continuous Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD)
  • • Heavy-duty SDR-11 HDPE conduit placement & duct-integrity proofing
  • • Tier-22 traffic-rated precast polymer handholes and vaults
  • • High-volume pneumatic cable blowing & jetting

High-Tension Aerial Cabling & Pole Mounting

Engineered utility pole attachments and overlashing designed to endure severe Western Canada wind vectors, ice loadings, and thermal cycling without fiber attenuation.

  • • Extra High-Strength (EHS) galvanized messenger strand stringing
  • • All-Dielectric Self-Supporting (ADSS) dielectric span deployment
  • • Utility pole attachment permit compliance & height clearance verification
  • • Dead-end tensioning, down-guy anchoring & snow/ice sag calculation

Core-Alignment Fusion Splicing

Precision single-fiber and ribbon fusion splicing executed in clean climate-controlled splicing labs with typical insertion loss held below 0.02 dB per junction.

  • • Single-Mode (OS2) and High-Count Ribbon (up to 864ct) splicing
  • • Hermetically sealed IP68 dome enclosures (FOSC / Coyote)
  • • Fiber distribution panel (FDP) termination & rack dressing
  • • Cleanroom-grade end-face inspection to IEC 61300-3-35

Bi-Directional OTDR Certification & Power Testing

Comprehensive Tier-1 optical power testing and Tier-2 bi-directional OTDR trace characterization ensuring carrier-grade acceptance standards and long-term transmission integrity.

  • • Multi-wavelength testing at 1310 nm, 1550 nm, and 1625 nm
  • • Optical return loss (ORL) characterization & splice verification
  • • Complete Telcordia .SOR trace files & engineering documentation
  • • 24/7 emergency cut repair truck rolls for fiber breaks
